# Spokane Deep Dive

Watch this six-part demonstration of HASTE using the Spokane example. The series
covers labeling, building embeddings, editing predictions, and understanding
outputs, with a bonus episode on the Open Data Catalog.

Allow about 17 minutes for the full series. Watch the episodes in order for the
complete walkthrough, or choose a topic below. Each video includes English
captions, available through the player's caption controls.
